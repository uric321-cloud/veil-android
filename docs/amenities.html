Pet policy

Your pets are welcome here.

Up to two pets per apartment. Designated play areas, waste stations and pet-friendly walking paths throughout the community.

How pet screening works

Luxe Park partners with PetScreening.com. Every applicant and resident creates a profile, with or without a pet. Residents with pets receive a FIDO / Paw Score that determines acceptance and any fees. Profiles renew annually, and all pets must be spayed or neutered and up to date on vaccinations.

Pet fee and pet rent

Pet fee is a one-time, non-refundable fee paid before move-in, set by your pet's score.

Pet rent is a monthly amount added to your lease, also set by your pet's score. Applicants cover the PetScreening profile fee. Call the office for current amounts and service-animal questions.
